§ 1. Purpose

To establish a systematic process for the Sovereign Architect to review the Quality Management System at planned intervals, ensuring its continuing suitability, adequacy, effectiveness, and alignment with the Kingdom's strategic direction.

"The Architect reviews. The Architect decides. The Architect seals or sends back. This is the gate-lock — nothing ships without it, nothing changes without it."
§ 2. Scope

This procedure applies to all management review activities within the Kingdom of Kwan QMS, including but not limited to:

  • Campaign-level reviews (at campaign close or major milestone)
  • Periodic reviews (quarterly or as determined by the Architect)
  • Triggered reviews (significant non-conformance, strategic shift, or external change)
  • All QMS processes, bricks, and governance documents

§ 6. Review Inputs (ISO 9.3.2)

The following inputs shall be compiled by Ocelot (CEO) and presented to the Architect prior to or during the management review:

📥 Mandatory Inputs
  • Status of actions from previous reviews
  • Changes in external/internal issues relevant to QMS
  • QMS performance and effectiveness data
  • Resource adequacy assessment
  • Risk and opportunity register status
  • Opportunities for improvement
📥 Kingdom-Specific Inputs
  • Campaign Status JSON (living whiteboard)
  • Stanley audit summary (all findings, closure rates)
  • Keila consultant recommendations
  • Attunement Score trends (AT-201)
  • Drift Detection log (FT-101)
  • Metabolism Rate throughput (PT-301)
  • Misalignment Registry status
  • CAPA log — open/closed/effectiveness verified
  • Conn log summaries (session records)
  • Stakeholder feedback (if applicable)

§ 9. Decision Matrix
Architect's Decision Framework
ConditionDecisionAction
QMS effective, objectives met, no significant findings CONTINUE Maintain current course. Log review. Set next review date.
Minor gaps identified, objectives partially met IMPROVE Issue improvement actions. Assign owners. Track to closure.
Significant non-conformance, systemic pattern detected CORRECT Issue CAPA. Root cause analysis required. Follow-up review scheduled.
Strategic misalignment, external change, scope shift REDIRECT Update policy/objectives. Revise scope. Launch new campaign if needed.
Critical failure, sovereignty at risk HALT Stop all campaigns. Emergency review. Architect-only decision.
