Brick 8 — Internal Audit Procedure

QMS-IAP-001 | Version 1.0 | ISO 9001:2015 Clause 9.2

Barcode: 8_01_08_1 | Campaign: FWO-009 | Session: S031

§ 1. Purpose

To establish a systematic, independent, and documented process for conducting internal audits of the Quality Management System (QMS) to determine conformity with planned arrangements, ISO 9001:2015 requirements, and organizational objectives.

"Fix the workflow, not the typo." — The audit exists to find systemic cracks, not to count commas. If the system is attuned, the commas fix themselves.
§ 2. Scope

This procedure applies to all processes and documented information within the approved QMS scope, including but not limited to:

  • All ratified Bricks (B1–B12)
  • Campaign execution and governance
  • Document control and filing procedures
  • Risk and opportunity assessments
  • Corrective action processes
  • Management review and continual improvement

The controlled QMS process inventory identifies applicable processes, owners, interfaces, and associated documented information. The list is maintained as the QMS changes.

§ 5. Audit Planning & Risk-Based Scheduling

An internal audit schedule shall be established at the beginning of each campaign cycle. Scheduling considers:

  • Importance of the process
  • Results of previous audits
  • Risk assessment outcomes (per QMS-ROA-001)
  • Changes affecting the QMS
  • Nonconformities, customer feedback, incidents, performance trends

Conducts the audit using the coin-flip methodology (3-rung ladder). Documents findings with evidence. Reports conclusions. Shall not audit own work without documented impartial arrangement.
Tier 2 — Keila (Consultant)
Reviews audit findings for completeness, accuracy, and ISO alignment. Validates methodology. Advisory — does not replace auditor's conclusion.

Evidence collection: document review, process observation, interviews, system/data inspection, sampling. Distinguish verified fact from interpretation. Where evidence is insufficient, record the limitation.

§ 7. Auditor Independence & Impartiality
  • Conflict-of-interest check recorded before each audit begins
  • Auditors shall not audit their own work
  • If no suitable independent internal auditor available: arrange competent independent reviewer, external auditor, or document another credible impartial arrangement
  • Independence assessed separately from competence
Stanley auditing his own audit and finding it non-conforming is expected behavior, not a deficiency. It is, however, the reason the 3-Tier protocol exists. 🪞♾️
§ 8. Findings Classification
P0 — Critical
Non-conformity that could result in system failure
Immediate corrective action required
P1 — Major
Significant gap affecting QMS effectiveness
Corrective action within defined timeline
P2 — Minor
Observation or improvement opportunity
Noted for continual improvement

P0/P1/P2 are Kingdom priority labels, not ISO 9001 classifications. Each finding must identify the requirement, objective evidence, and conclusion. Priority is separate from conformity status.

§ 9. Corrective Action & Closure
  1. Correction: Immediate action to eliminate detected nonconformity
  2. Root Cause Analysis: Evaluate cause — not just the symptom
  3. Corrective Action: Action to prevent recurrence or occurrence elsewhere
  4. Implementation: Assigned to responsible party with target date
  5. Verification: Independent verifier reviews evidence of effectiveness
  6. Closure: Only when required evidence supports it — not by assertion
PriorityProposed TimelineNote
P0 Within 10 business days Kingdom rule — escalation if overdue
P1 Within 20 business days Kingdom rule — extensions documented
P2 Accepted, deferred, or declined with rationale Not every observation requires corrective action
"Fix the workflow, not the typo." — A deadline does not by itself establish effective action. Close the finding only when evidence supports closure. If ineffective, reopen.
§ 10. Audit Reporting

Issue report within 5 business days of audit completion. Report shall include:

  • Audit ID, objectives, scope, criteria, date, and status
  • Auditor(s), auditee(s), and documented independence check
  • Methods and material sampling limitations
  • Each finding: criterion, evidence, classification, linked reference
  • Unresolved disagreements, evidence limitations, scope restrictions
  • Required follow-up, responsible owner, due date, verification approach
  • Report author, reviewer, issue date, version, distribution record

Completeness review shall not permit coordinator to rewrite auditor's conclusion without evidence. All audit records filed per DCP-001 with appropriate barcodes.

§ 11. Auditor Competence
  • Evidence of competence: relevant training, demonstrated audit practice, QMS knowledge, evaluation of prior performance
  • Competence reviewed when audit performance, scope changes, or new requirements indicate need
  • Attendance at training alone is not conclusive proof of competence
  • Independence assessed separately from competence
§ 12. Management Review & Continual Improvement
  • Audit results presented to Architect during campaign checkpoints
  • Trends in findings analyzed for systemic issues
  • Program effectiveness reviewed annually
  • Inputs to management review: coverage vs plan, significant results, repeat findings, overdue actions, resource constraints

Record decisions and actions — the phrase "continual improvement" is not proof that improvement occurred.

§ 13. Minimum Audit Record Set

For each audit, the record set must answer:

  1. What was intended to be audited, against which criteria, and why?
  2. Who conducted the audit, and how were competence and independence established?
  3. What evidence was examined, and how did it support each conclusion?
  4. What was reported, to whom, and when?
  5. Who responded to each nonconformity, what action was taken, and what evidence established effectiveness?
  6. How were incomplete actions, overdue items, and unresolved disagreements handled?
If the retained records cannot answer these questions, the audit record is incomplete and the gap shall be addressed.
