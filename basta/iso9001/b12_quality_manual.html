§ 2. Quality Policy (ISO 5.2) — Brick 1

It is the policy of the Kingdom of Kwan to provide quality AI-integrated development solutions and sovereign intelligence frameworks through disciplined process engineering, collaborative intelligence, and continuous improvement.

This commitment is achieved through the involvement of all contributors at every level of the organization, including but not limited to human architects, AI agents, consultants, and auditors — and by meeting or exceeding the requirements and expectations of all stakeholders.

  • Satisfy applicable requirements including but not limited to ISO 9001:2015 standards
  • Pursue continual improvement of the quality management system
  • Maintain sovereignty, transparency, and model-agnostic design in all deliverables
  • Ensure all processes follow the Kwan-Gani-Basta cycle: ideate, build, ship

§ 4. Scope of QMS (ISO 4.3) — Brick 3

This Quality Management System applies to the design, development, testing, deployment, and continuous improvement of AI-integrated development tools, frameworks, and publications produced by the Kingdom of Kwan. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • AI agent orchestration and RAG pipeline development
  • Web-based publications, notebooks, and interactive content
  • Process engineering frameworks and governance documentation
  • Campaign-based project management and delivery pipelines
  • Quality documentation including policies, procedures, and work instructions

Exclusions

  • Third-party AI model training (we consume, we do not train base models)
  • Hardware manufacturing
  • Cloud infrastructure operations managed by third-party providers

§ 9. Internal Audit Procedure (ISO 9.2) — Brick 8

Internal audits are conducted by Stanley (Auditor) on every brick before ratification. The audit process uses a 3-Rung Ladder (coin flip) technique for deep-dive assessments and standard D1-D2-D3 review cycles for routine audits.

  • Rung 1: Surface scan — identify gaps, log findings
  • Rung 2: Stress test — challenge assumptions, test registrar readiness
  • Rung 3: Constructive contribution — propose solutions, not just findings

§ 10. Corrective & Preventive Action (ISO 10.2) — Brick 9

The CAPA process ensures all non-conformances, audit findings, and observed misalignments are systematically addressed through root cause analysis, corrective action implementation, and effectiveness verification.

  • Trigger: Audit finding, drift detection, stakeholder feedback, or self-identified gap
  • Process: Identify → Analyze Root Cause → Implement Correction → Verify Effectiveness
  • Closure: Evidence-based — closed by doing, not by writing
