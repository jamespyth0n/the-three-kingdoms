§ 1. Purpose

To establish and maintain a documented information control system that ensures all Kingdom assets are created, identified, reviewed, approved, stored, protected, retrieved, retained, and disposed of in accordance with ISO 9001:2015 Clause 7.5.

A document without a barcode is a napkin. A napkin without a shelf is spaghetti. Spaghetti without a system is chaos. This brick is the system.
§ 2. Scope

This procedure applies to all documented information required by the QMS, including but not limited to:

  • Quality policies, mission/vision statements, and objectives
  • Risk and opportunity assessments
  • Roles, responsibilities, and authorities
  • Campaign artifacts, connection logs, and session records
  • Technical specifications, code documentation, and build records
  • External-origin documents referenced by the QMS

§ 4. Document Identification & Barcode Convention

All controlled documents shall carry:

  • Barcode: Dewey-style semantic identifier (X_XX_XX_X) mapped to the Kingdom's 7-layer filing model
  • Title: Descriptive, unambiguous
  • Version: Semantic versioning (vX.Y) with ISO 8601 timestamps
  • Author/Owner: Identified role (Ocelot / Keila / Stanley / Architect)
  • Status: Lifecycle state per § 5
§ 5. Document Lifecycle States
DRAFT (D1)
Initial proposal. Not authorized for controlled use.
REVISION (D2)
Revised text awaiting review and approval.
AUDIT REVIEWED (D3)
Audit complete. Pending final approval.
RATIFIED
Approved by Architect. Effective and controlled.
SUPERSEDED
Replaced by later revision. Retained as historical.
ARCHIVED
Retained for historical, legal, or contractual purpose.

§ 10. Retention & Disposition
  • Active Documents: Retained in authoritative storage per retention schedule
  • Superseded: Retained as historical record with SUPERSEDED status
  • Disposition: No controlled document destroyed without Architect authorization
  • Legal Holds: Records under investigation or dispute preserved until formally released
§ 11. Change Control
  • All changes require a new version increment
  • MAJOR (structural/scope) → Full review pipeline
  • MINOR (editorial/formatting) → Ocelot + Architect approval
  • Prior revision marked SUPERSEDED in register; retained per retention rule
  • Change record: affected doc, proposed change, reason, impact, reviewer, decision, effective date
§ 12. External-Origin Documents
  • External documents referenced by QMS identified, recorded, and controlled for currency
  • Source, version, and applicability verified before incorporation
  • A copy in the Kingdom Library does not by itself establish it is current or authorized
§ 13. Metabolism Protocol
  • Campaign status JSON updated at each brick completion (Eat → Digest → Poop)
  • Living whiteboard reflects current state — completed items removed, new items added
  • Triggered by: brick completion, audit closure, ratification, or scope change
