§ 1. Purpose

This document defines the scope of the Quality Management System (QMS) for the Kingdom of Kwan — a sovereign AI-integrated development environment (IDE) designed, built, and operated by the Sovereign Architect.

§ 2. Organization
Entity Description
Organization The Kingdom of Kwan
Operating Entity Office of the Sovereign Architect
Structure Three Kingdoms — Kwan (Research & Publication), Gani (Development & Augmentation), Basta (Governance & Delivery)
§ 3. Scope Statement

The QMS applies to the design, development, testing, deployment, and continuous improvement of AI-integrated development tools, frameworks, and publications produced by the Kingdom of Kwan.

This includes, but is not limited to:

  • AI-integrated development tools — including the Ocelot Bridge IDE, RAG pipelines, and model-agnostic orchestration layers
  • Knowledge management systems — including the Library of Alexandria, Kingdom Index, and document control procedures
  • Publication and research outputs — including notebooks, monographs, articles, and campaign deliverables produced through the Three Kingdoms framework
  • Campaign execution pipelines — including the Campaign Law Protocol, build toolchains, and formalized work orders (FWOs)
§ 4. Boundaries
✅ In Scope
All deliverables produced under the Kingdom's governance, regardless of platform, model, or deployment target. This includes all artifacts produced under FWO campaigns, all documents governed by DCP-001, all code, templates, and toolchains within the Library of Alexandria, all ally interactions that produce ratified deliverables, and all conn_logs, ocelot_thoughts, and session artifacts filed per the 7-layer OSI filing structure.
❌ Excluded
Third-party LLM training data and internal model weights (we consume, we do not train base models). External hosting infrastructure not under Architect control. Personal communications not filed as Kingdom artifacts. Experimental servilletas (napkin drafts) until formally promoted to D1 status. Hardware manufacturing. Cloud infrastructure operations managed by third-party providers.
🔄 Flexibility Clause
The scope may be expanded or refined as the Kingdom evolves. New domains, tools, and capabilities shall be incorporated through the established change management process. "Not limited to" applies throughout — the Kingdom grows, the scope grows with it.

§ 8. Justification for Exclusions

Per ISO 9001:2015 Clause 4.3, exclusions are justified where:

  • The organization does not control the process (third-party LLM internals)
  • The exclusion does not affect the ability to ensure conformity of products and services (napkin drafts are pre-QMS by design — they enter the system only upon D1 promotion)

This document shall be reviewed at the start of every major campaign, after every 10 sessions, or whenever the Architect determines a revision is necessary — per DCP-001 version control procedures.
