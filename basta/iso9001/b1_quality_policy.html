§ 1. Policy Statement

It is the policy of the Kingdom of Kwan to provide quality AI-integrated development solutions and sovereign intelligence frameworks through disciplined process engineering, collaborative intelligence, and continuous improvement.

This commitment is achieved through the involvement of all contributors at every level of the organization, including but not limited to human architects, AI agents, consultants, and auditors — and by meeting or exceeding the requirements and expectations of all stakeholders.

§ 2. Guiding Principle
"Doing the right things, right the first time."

— Inherited from Kisho Sakata Electronics Philippines Inc. Carried forward into the Kingdom as foundational DNA.
§ 3. The Three Pillars of Quality
Pillar I — KWAN (Intuition → Raw Knowing)
Quality begins with the napkin. The first sketch. The gut feeling. Kwan is the courage to start before the plan is perfect — because the plan is never perfect. Quality is the discipline to capture, refine, and ship.
Pillar II — GANI (Augmentation → Structured Knowing)
Quality grows through the notebook. The structured process. The campaign protocol. The 666 review cycle. Gani is the discipline to take the raw napkin and subject it to scrutiny — by the Consultant, by the Auditor, by the Architect. Quality is not a solo act.
Pillar III — BASTA (Context → Settled Knowing)
Quality ships through the journal. The final artifact. The sealed document. Basta is the discipline to declare "done" — not because it is perfect, but because it has been tested, reviewed, and found sufficient. Quality is the courage to ship.
§ 4. Quality Commitments
  • 01 Customer Focus — The Architect's vision is the north star. Every artifact serves the mission.
  • 02 Leadership — The CEO/Management (Ocelot) maintains the QMS and ensures every campaign follows protocol.
  • 03 Engagement of People — Every ally is empowered to challenge, review, and improve.
  • 04 Process Approach — The Campaign Law Protocol governs all builds. No brick ships without the pipeline.
  • 05 Improvement — The Metabolism Protocol ensures continuous learning. We eat, digest, poop, and grow.
  • 06 Evidence-Based Decision Making — The Kingdom Index, conn_logs, and campaign status JSONs are the evidence. We don't guess. We trace.
  • 07 Relationship Management — The allies are partners. The 666 review cycle ensures every voice is heard.
