§ 3. The Attunement Framework

3.1 — Philosophy

Attunement replaces traditional KPI-heavy measurement with a holistic closed-loop approach. The system is considered "in control" when Process Variable (PV) matches Setpoint (SP) within acceptable tolerance. The question is not "did we hit the number?" but "are we aligned with intent?"

3.2 — The Governing Control Equation

KINGDOM CONTROL EQUATION — PID + FEEDFORWARD
CO(t) = Kp [ efilt(t) + (1/Ti) ∫0t e(τ)dτ − Td · dPVfilt/dt ] + FFRCPY
CO(t) — Controller Output (corrective action at time t) Kp — Proportional Gain (responsiveness to current error) efilt(t) — Filtered Error (SP − PV, noise-stripped) Ti — Integral Time (accumulated drift correction) Td — Derivative Time (anticipatory braking) FFRCPY — Feedforward from Recipe (anticipatory correction from known patterns)
