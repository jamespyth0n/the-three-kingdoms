§ 4. Risk Assessment — FMEA Methodology
Severity (S)
1–10: Impact on QMS integrity, delivery, or sovereignty
Occurrence (O)
1–10: Likelihood of occurrence
Detection (D)
1–10: Ability to detect before impact (10 = undetectable)

RPN = S × O × D  |  Critical ≥ 300  |  Major 150–299  |  Moderate 80–149
