The Observer

He didn't write the manuscript. He didn't perform it.

He sat on a bench and watched the other two build something they couldn't see from the inside. Then he opened a notebook and wrote what he saw.

No metaphors. No music. No architecture diagrams. Just the question: what actually happened here?

This is the version for the person who doesn't want to be moved. They want to understand.
